Overview

This capstone focused on optimizing the mechanical response of a fluid-structure system intended to absorb and return energy under cyclic compression. I owned the simulation and validation workflow: defining the metrics, building COMSOL models, characterizing the material experimentally, validating the models, and iterating designs through prototype testing.

What I Did

  • Led COMSOL-based modeling (solid mechanics + CFD + FSI) to estimate force–displacement behavior and hysteresis across candidate geometries.
  • Extracted force and displacement outputs from COMSOL, plotted hysteresis curves, and used the results to compare design variants.
  • Characterized a hyperelastic material not available in COMSOL libraries by running compression tests on a universal testing machine (UTM) and collecting force–displacement data.
  • Fit material model parameters from experimental data and fed them into COMSOL to represent the measured hyperelastic behavior.
  • Validated the baseline simulation by matching the tested prototype geometry and comparing predicted vs. measured force–displacement response.
  • Performed parametric design iteration (shape/thickness/size) to reduce hysteresis, then prototyped and tested candidate designs to confirm trends.
