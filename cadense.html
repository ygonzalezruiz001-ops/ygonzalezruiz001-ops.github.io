Project Overview

Designed and optimized a performance footwear system using coupled structural mechanics, CFD, and fluid-structure interaction (FSI) simulations in COMSOL Multiphysics.

The objective was to understand how dynamic impact forces interact with internal fluid regions and structural geometry to improve energy absorption and performance under loading.

Technical Scope

  • Performed solid mechanics simulation for impact loading conditions.
  • Conducted CFD analysis to model internal fluid behavior.
  • Coupled solid + fluid domains using FSI for realistic response prediction.
  • Optimized geometry for improved stress distribution and energy absorption.
  • Validated simulation trends through physical impact testing.

Impact Testing & Validation

Designed and built a custom impact testing apparatus to validate simulation results.

Physical drop testing allowed comparison between predicted stress distribution, deformation patterns, and real-world behavior.
