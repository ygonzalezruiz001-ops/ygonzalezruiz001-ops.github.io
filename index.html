Thermal Systems Architecture for LV Cabinet (Hyliion — Generator Platform)

Forced Convection • System Curves • CFD • Environmental Filtration • Integration

  • Designed a forced-convection cooling strategy to keep LV cabinet temperatures within reliability limits.
  • Built an iterative sizing model coupling airflow requirement + pressure drop + fan power heat add-back until convergence.
  • Developed a cabinet system curve (ΔP vs V̇) using CFD, anchored by instrumentation/measurements from a prior platform configuration.
  • Selected fan at the true operating point (system curve × supplier fan curve) while respecting electrical power constraints.
  • Integrated intake/exhaust geometry and filtration strategy for humid/marine-adjacent environments and tight packaging.
