Thermal Systems Architecture for LV Cabinet (Hyliion — Generator Platform)

Forced Convection • System Curves • Fan & Filter Selection • Validation Planning

  • Defined the cooling approach and presented an end-to-end plan for LV cabinet thermal control.
  • Calculated required airflow (volumetric flow rate) from heat load and allowable temperature rise.
  • Estimated system pressure drop and used ΔP–V̇ to select a fan at the operating point within electrical power constraints.
  • Selected a filtration strategy aligned to the expected operating environment and packaging constraints.

Containerized Energy Generator Frames (Hyliion — 2 MW & 800 kW)

Structural Development • Modal/Static FEA • Transport + Handling Loads • Weldment Design

  • Owned structural verification (ANSYS) for two containerized generator frames (2 MW and 800 kW), starting from preliminary weldment CAD.
  • Drove design-to-requirements iterations: updated the structure in CAD (bracing/gussets/thickness tuning) and re-ran FEA to close gaps to targets.
  • Executed shipping + handling load cases: modal frequency avoidance, 5g transport accelerations, crane lift (sling angle), forklift pickup, and stiffness checks.
  • Improved dynamic stiffness through targeted joint stiffening and bracing (raised minimum natural frequency toward program targets).
  • Completed weld sizing to AWS D1.1 using FEA-extracted forces/moments and supported drawing release for prototype fabrication.

Hydrogen Generation System Prototyping (Capella Partners)

Mechanical Design • Prototyping • SolidWorks CAD/CFD • Sealing/Isolation Testing • DFM/DFA

  • Reconstructed and documented an existing prototype in SolidWorks and ran preliminary CFD to identify recirculation/stagnation regions that informed the next iteration.

Hypershoe Optimization (Cadense Capstone)

COMSOL Multiphysics • FSI • Hyperelastic Materials • Testing & Validation • Rapid Iteration

  • Led COMSOL-based FSI + solid mechanics modeling to quantify hysteresis (energy loss) and energy return across candidate designs.
  • Characterized a non-library hyperelastic material via compression tests; extracted force–displacement data and fit material model parameters for simulation.
  • Validated the simulation workflow by matching COMSOL predictions to physical test data before expanding to new geometries.
  • Ran design iterations (shape/thickness/size) to reduce hysteresis, then prototyped and tested concepts to close the loop between analysis and experiments.
  • Supported rapid testing using a benchtop impact/compression rig; benchmarked prototypes and achieved up to ~82% energy return in testing.
  • Awarded “Most Technical Project” (top recognition among ~30 capstone teams).
