Overview

Worked on a lab-scale hydrogen generation system (alkaline electrolysis / KOH-based) where reliable separation and isolation of hydrogen and oxygen streams was critical. My role spanned CAD ownership, concept-level CFD for documentation and design insight, hands-on fabrication, and validation testing to reduce technical and safety risk.

Prototype Iterations

Prototype v1 (existing hardware → documentation + flow insight)

  • Created a full SolidWorks CAD model for an existing lab prototype that lacked formal documentation.
  • Ran basic CFD to identify likely stagnation/recirculation near geometric corners, informing improvements for the next iteration.

Prototype v2 (redesign + build + isolation validation)

  • Designed the next iteration from scratch in SolidWorks, incorporating R&D feedback while keeping the assembly manufacturable.
  • Ran basic CFD to compare flow-path behavior and guide geometry changes for smoother transport through the system.
  • Machined and assembled the prototype in-house (Al 6061, gaskets/rubber, and acrylic sections for visualization).
  • Validated internal isolation and external leak resistance with a dyed-water endurance test (~24 hours).
  • Prepared preliminary joint/fastener sizing calculations for a future stainless/metal pressurized build (design intent ~400 psi).

Prototype v3 (new architecture → CAD ownership + concept demonstrator)

  • Owned a new concept architecture (initiated by leadership): defined fluid path, assembly approach, symmetry, maintainability, and part-count reduction.
  • Produced full CAD + basic CFD and built an FDM-printed concept model for fit/assembly visualization and design reviews.

Safety-Critical Example: Hydrogen Sensor Fitting

  • Designed a custom fitting to integrate a hydrogen sensor between the generator system and a storage reservoir.
  • Performed an early safety check (compressed air + soap-bubble leak test) and detected leakage before any hydrogen use.
  • Investigated gas permeability risk in FDM parts and redesigned the fitting as a machined 6061-T6 component.
  • Re-tested the aluminum version to confirm leak-tight performance; used in lab service with no safety incidents.
