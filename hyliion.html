Overview

Owned structural design and verification for containerized, megawatt-scale generator frames through detailed design review and release. Scope included load-case definition, architecture optimization, weldment detailing, and manufacturability alignment with fabrication partners to ensure the design met stiffness, strength, and integration requirements.

Structural Optimization

  • Applied a “every part must earn its keep” approach to remove non-essential components while preserving load paths and assembly intent.
  • Verified performance in ANSYS using stress, deflection, and strain-energy review across 5g transport and 3g operational conditions.
  • Iterated designs with rapid re-analysis to confirm margins and stiffness targets after each simplification cycle.

Dynamics / Resonance De-risking

  • Increased the minimum natural frequency by ~4× using targeted bracing and joint stiffening (gussets), prioritizing stiffness-to-mass efficiency.
  • Intent: reduce resonance risk by separating structural modes from rotating-equipment excitation bands.

Weld Verification (AWS D1.1)

Weld sizing was verified with hand calculations driven by ANSYS reaction forces and moments at weld interfaces. FEA outputs were mapped into normal force N, shear V, bending moments M_x, M_y, and torsion T, then checked against AWS D1.1 allowables.

Fillet weld basics
$$t = 0.707\,w,\qquad A_t = t\,L_{\mathrm{eff}}$$
Throat t from fillet leg size w; effective throat area based on effective weld length.
Stresses from loads (weld group)
$$\sigma = \frac{N}{A_t} + \frac{M_x}{S_x} + \frac{M_y}{S_y}\qquad \tau = \frac{V}{A_t} + \frac{T\,r}{J}$$
Normal = axial + bending; shear = direct + torsional. Weld-group properties S_x, S_y, J depend on geometry.
Combined check + allowable
$$\sigma_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{\sigma^2+3\tau^2}\;\;\le\;\;\tau_{\mathrm{allow}},\qquad \tau_{\mathrm{allow}}\approx 0.30\,F_{\mathrm{EXX}}$$
Example: E70XX ⇒ \(F_{\mathrm{EXX}}=70\,\text{ksi}\) ⇒ \(\tau_{\mathrm{allow}}\approx 21\,\text{ksi}\). Solve for weld size w to meet utilization targets.
CJP / groove weld (equivalent stress form)
$$\sigma_{vm}=\sqrt{\sigma_{\mathrm{total}}^2+3\tau_{\mathrm{total}}^2}\;\;\le\;\;\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}$$
Used for joints specified as CJP/groove where applicable; stresses derived from the same mapped force/moment set.

Portfolio note: formulas shown are representative of the workflow; proprietary joint details and exact load values are intentionally omitted.

Supplier / Fabrication Coordination

  • Released drawings, cut lists, and material requirements with clear tolerances and acceptance expectations.
  • Aligned weld callouts and joint accessibility with fabrication capabilities (fit-up, process access, and realistic shop sequencing).
  • Supported supplier evaluation around lead time, cost, and certification constraints.
