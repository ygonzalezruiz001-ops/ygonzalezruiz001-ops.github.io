Overview

Leading structural design and analysis for containerized megawatt-scale energy generator systems from concept through detailed design review. Responsible for frame architecture optimization, AWS D1.1 weld stress analysis, supplier coordination, and DFM.

Structural Optimization

  • Applied “every part must earn its keep” philosophy to remove non-load-carrying components.
  • Validated load paths with ANSYS using von Mises + strain energy reviews under 5g transport and 3g operational cases.
  • Reran models after removals to ensure margins and stiffness requirements were preserved.

Dynamics / Resonance Avoidance

  • Raised minimum natural frequency from 8 Hz to 31 Hz via targeted X-bracing and gusseting.
  • Goal: avoid rotating equipment excitation frequencies to prevent resonance.

Weld Analysis + DFM

  • Performed AWS D1.1 weld stress analysis using forces/moments extracted from FEA.
  • Redesigned load paths using stiffeners instead of simply increasing weld size (cost/weight control).
  • Coordinated with fabrication partner to confirm CJP feasibility, access, and realistic shop processes.

Supplier Coordination

  • Released drawings, cut lists, and material specs with clear tolerances and acceptance expectations.
  • Evaluated alternate suppliers (lead time, cost, certifications) and managed delivery schedules.
